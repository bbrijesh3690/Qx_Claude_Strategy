# Phase 2 hypothesis-generation prompt

Sent to Fable 5.1 (high effort) on a separate account, 2026-09-15. The model
was given rules and constraints only, never the dataset, so its proposals
cannot be mined from the bars they will be screened on. Its output is
recorded as-is in `phase2-hypotheses-response.md` before any screening.

---

```text
I'm running a pre-registered research program to test whether any rule-based setup on Quotex OTC binary options can reach a high hit rate. I need you to propose hypotheses to screen. You will not see the data, and that is deliberate: every idea must come from a stated mechanism, not from pattern-mining.

## The market
- Quotex OTC binary options on 9 synthetic pairs: AUD/NZD, GBP/NZD, NZD/CHF, NZD/USD, USD/BDT, USD/BRL, USD/IDR, USD/MXN, USD/PHP. All are "(OTC)": the feed is generated by the broker, who is also the counterparty. It trades 24/7.
- A trade is CALL or PUT with a fixed expiry of 1, 5 or 15 minutes. Win = payout 90% of stake; loss = whole stake; tie refunds.
- Break-even hit rate at 90% payout is 52.6%. The target is ~75%.

## What a hypothesis can see
- Only closed 1-minute OHLC bars (time, open, high, low, close), up to the decision bar, per asset. No volume, no tick stream, no order book, no news, no payout, no other assets' bars.
- Decision at the close of bar i. Entry at bar i+1's open. Exit at the close of bar i+expiry.
- One open position per asset at a time (no overlapping trades). Series are split at data gaps, so a rule never sees across one.
- A rule may return null (stand aside). Most minutes should be null: a 75% setup will be rare.

## Facts about this data you may use
These are descriptive facts from data-quality checks, not results of pattern searches:
- Always-CALL, always-PUT and random direction all score ~50% at 1/5/15m; no asset has a directional drift.
- The next bar opens at, or within a tick of, the previous close (no overnight-style gaps).
- The feed contains rare "shock" candles: a single 1-minute bar moving 0.2%–5.9% inside its body (open at prior close), roughly 2–3 per asset per week, after which price continues from the new level. One verified genuine gap exists.
- Flat (tie) outcomes are ~1.2% at 1m, 0.5% at 5m, 0.3% at 15m.
- Training data is ~4.5 days × 9 assets (~58,000 1m bars). Screening a 75% edge needs ~71 decided trades, so a rule must fire at least ~16 times per day across all 9 assets to be testable now.

## Already tested and dead — do not re-propose, including reweighted versions
Measured on 26,734 clean rows: 50.4% overall. None of these components beat 50% on its own:
- 15-minute candle direction (forming or closed bar)
- 5-minute trend (last 5m close vs prior)
- 1m RSI(14) extremes (≤32 / ≥68), and RSI momentum around 50
- Proximity to a 20-bar 1m high/low range (<15% of range)
- A weighted confluence score of the above. Higher scores did WORSE (2.5–3.0 → 51.5%, 4.5–5.0 → 47.6%).
Untested and allowed: 15-minute support/resistance rejection (a 1m wick pierces a 15m-derived level but closes back).

The program stops permanently after 10 consecutive hypotheses fail. Every hypothesis you propose spends that budget. Weak or redundant ideas are costly. I want FEW, well-reasoned, genuinely different hypotheses, not a long list.

## What I want from you
Propose between 4 and 8 hypotheses, ranked best-first by your honest belief they will beat 52.6% out of sample. For each:

1. id — short kebab-case, prefixed h001, h002, …
2. title — one line
3. mechanism — why this edge would exist in a BROKER-GENERATED synthetic feed specifically (how such feeds are typically built: random walks with drift/volatility regimes, mean-reverting processes, injected jumps, smoothing, anti-trader bias, time-based parameter changes). Argue it; don't assert it.
4. rule — exact, unambiguous, with every threshold fixed now. No "tune later". No parameters to optimise.
5. expiries — only those the mechanism justifies (1, 5 and/or 15)
6. expected fire rate — per asset per day, with reasoning
7. predictedRate — your honest hit-rate estimate (not the 75% target unless you truly believe it)
8. refutation — what result would show the mechanism is wrong
9. overlap — which other proposed hypotheses it is correlated with, if any
10. code — a JavaScript function in exactly this form:

     id: "h001-...", title: "...", statement: "<mechanism + rule, 2-4 sentences>",

     registeredAt: "2026-09-15"
   };

Finally, give an overall assessment in under 150 words: your honest probability that ANY rule on 1m OHLC of a broker-generated OTC feed reaches 75% out of sample, and which single hypothesis you'd screen first if only one were allowed. Be blunt; I would rather stop early than chase noise.
```
